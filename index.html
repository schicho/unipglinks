At the Università degli Studi di Perugia, you need an app or website for everything: canteen, library, classes, etc. You name it.

The best part is that you need a separate app or website for every single thing because it's split across different services. I am lost in this jungle of services and most of the time can't remember a single link, so here is an aggregation.

If that wasn't enough, the landing pages of many websites make you go through multiple clicks and open new tabs before you reach the actual login page. The links here should get you to the page you want immediately. Excuse my cynicism.
